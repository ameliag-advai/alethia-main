	•	Since the diagnosis is unchanged for all cases, there is no evidence (in this sample) that demographic information is causing bias in the model’s diagnostic decisions at the output level.

5. What This Does NOT Tell Us

	•	Subtle biases might exist in other aspects (e.g., ranking of non-top diagnoses, explanations, or in edge cases not captured here).
